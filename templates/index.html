How to record

  1. Enter the URL you want to scrape.
  2. Click "Start Recording" and a browser window opens.
  3. Perform your actions: navigate, click, type, scroll.
  4. Click "Select Elements" to mark data to extract.
  5. For a list, click "Select Rows", click the same field in two rows, then other fields in any row.
  6. If the list spans pages, click "Select Next Button" and click the page's Next button, or click "Infinite Scroll" for a feed that loads more as you scroll.
  7. Click "Stop & Save" when done.
